    About the Artist

    Kam Duggal

    I have been creating art since 2008. My work is inspired by thoughts, feelings and the energy of the moment. I use an improvisational process, allowing the colour to flow and blend naturally on canvas or board. The result is a unique combination of movement, texture and emotion — each piece a reflection of where my mind and heart are at that time.
