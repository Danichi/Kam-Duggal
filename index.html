The work

An improvisational painter working in acrylic and oil on canvas and board. Many pieces are made without a brush at all.

Meet the artist

I have been creating art since 2008, inspired by thoughts, feelings and the energy of the moment. I let the paint flow, let each colour mix as it happens, and push what I'm feeling onto the canvas.

    The process

    Poured, tilted, left to move.

    Vase · Acrylic and oil on board01
    1. 01

      The surface

      Every piece starts on canvas or board. Some are small ten-inch studies. Others are four-foot canvases that need the whole floor.

    2. 02

      The colour

      Acrylics, and sometimes oil, chosen for how they'll move against each other and not just how they look in the jar.

    3. 03

      The flow

      The paint is poured and guided across the surface. Kam lets each colour meet and mix on its own, so no two pieces can ever come out the same.

      “I experiment with the flow of the paint on the canvas or board and let each colour mix as it happens.”
    4. 04

      The moment

      Each painting holds on to whatever Kam was thinking and feeling that day. Then it's signed, and it's finished.

      “In each original piece I try to bring out my thoughts in the moment.”
