Hi Kam,

All three sorted, and they are on the site now.

**The photos will upload.** You were right, and it was worse than it looked. The
site was actually telling you what was wrong each time, but it was printing the
message on the bar along the bottom of the screen, which the Paintings window
was sitting on top of. So it looked like nothing happened at all. The message
now appears inside the Paintings window where you cannot miss it.

While I was in there I widened what it accepts. Photos straight off an iPhone
work now, so do TIFFs, and anything too large is shrunk down for you instead of
being refused. If a photo still will not go, it will now tell you exactly why
rather than sitting there.

**Likes and comments are in.** Open any painting from Original Artwork and look
under Buy a Print. There is a heart with a count, and a Comments button. Anyone
can like a painting once, and leave their name and a few words about it.

Nothing anyone writes goes onto your website until you say so. When a comment
comes in, a number appears next to **Comments** in your editor bar. Press it and
you get everything waiting, each with **Approve** and **Delete**. Approve and it
is on the painting straight away. Delete and it is gone for good and nobody ever
saw it. Anything already on the site is listed underneath, so you can take
something down later if you change your mind.

One thing to know: there is no email telling you a comment has arrived, you have
to sign in and look. If you would rather it emailed you, say the word and I will
add it.

**Linking new artwork to your print shop.** Press **Paintings** in the editor
bar and open any piece. There is a box that reads *Print shop link for this
painting*. Go to that painting's page on your Pixels shop, copy the address out
of the top of the browser, and paste it in there. That is exactly what the
**Buy a Print** button uses, so from then on it takes people to that painting
rather than to your shop's front page.

When you add a brand new painting it starts out pointing at your shop generally,
which is fine until the print listing exists. Once it does, paste the link in
and it is done. Remember to press **Publish changes** afterwards.
