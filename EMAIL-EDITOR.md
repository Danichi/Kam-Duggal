**Subject:** How to change your website yourself

Hi Kam,

Your site is built so you can run it yourself. No calling me to change a price, no
waiting a week to put a new painting up. Here is the whole thing in one go.

**Signing in**

Go to https://kam-duggal.pages.dev and look at the top right of the menu bar,
just past Contact. There is a small padlock. Click it and put in the password I
sent you separately. That is the only sign in on the site, and visitors never see
anything but the padlock.

Once you are in, a black bar appears along the bottom of the screen. That bar is
your control panel and only you can see it.

**Changing words**

Flick the switch that says **Edit mode**. Every bit of text you can change gets a
faint dotted line around it. Click into any of it and type, the same as writing an
email. Headings, paragraphs, the words under the paintings, your statement, the
questions and answers, the footer.

**Changing pictures**

In Edit mode, click any painting on the site and your files open. Pick a photo and
it goes straight in, resized for the web automatically so you do not have to think
about file sizes. If you prefer dragging, drag the photo from a folder onto the
painting instead. Both do the same thing.

This works on every picture: the big ones at the top, the four on the front page,
the whole gallery, the close-up, your own photo on the artist page, all of it.

**Adding a painting**

Press **Paintings** in the bottom bar. That is your whole collection in a list.
The button at the top says **Add a painting**: choose the photo, then fill in the
title, size and price on the row that appears. That is it, it is on the site.

In the same list you can:

- Change any title, medium, size or price
- Write a line about a painting, which shows when someone opens it
- Tick **Sold** so it stops being offered, or **Hide** to take it off the site
- Tick **Hero** for the paintings that cycle at the very top of the home page
- Tick **Featured** for the four on the front page
- Drag rows by the handle on the left to put the collection in your own order
- Remove a piece completely

**Moving things around**

Still in Edit mode, hover over any section of the page and a small **Move** handle
and a **Hide** button appear in its corner. Drag the handle to put sections in a
different order, or press Hide to take a section off the page. Hidden things stay
on your screen greyed out so you can always bring them back.

**Making it go live**

Nothing you do shows up on the website until you press **Publish changes**. Until
then it is only on your screen, so you can try things without any worry.

When you do press it, the site updates for everyone within about a second. Someone
opening your website right then sees your new painting or your new price. It is
properly live, not a request that comes to me.

If you publish something and change your mind, **Undo publish** puts it straight
back the way it was.

**What to send me instead**

Anything structural. A new page, a different layout, a change to how the site is
built. Send me a note and I will do it.

Everything about the art itself, the words, the photos, the prices, what is sold
and what order it all sits in, is yours whenever you want it.

Have a play with it. You cannot break anything that Undo publish or I cannot fix.
