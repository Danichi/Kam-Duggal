    The process

    Poured, tilted, left to move.

    Vase · Acrylic and oil on board01
    1. 01

      The surface

      Every piece starts on canvas or board. Some are small ten-inch studies. Others are four-foot canvases that need the whole floor.

    2. 02

      The colour

      Acrylics, and sometimes oil, chosen for how they will move against each other and not just how they look in the jar.

    3. 03

      The flow

      The paint is poured and guided across the surface. Kam lets each colour meet and mix on its own, so no two pieces can ever come out the same.

      “I experiment with the flow of the paint on the canvas or board and let each colour mix as it happens.”
    4. 04

      The moment

      Each painting holds on to whatever Kam was thinking and feeling that day. Then it is signed, and it is finished.

      “In each original piece I try to bring out my thoughts in the moment.”

    About the artist

    Kam Duggal

    I have been creating art since 2008. My work is inspired by thoughts, feelings and the energy of the moment. I use an improvisational process, letting the colour flow and blend naturally on canvas or board.

    The result is a combination of movement, texture and emotion. Each piece is a reflection of where my mind and heart were at that time.
